Reviewer setup

Pre-review glossary

SOURCE FACT
Persisted information obtained from an official source or its evidence record.
SYSTEM CLASSIFICATION
A bounded classification produced by the system; it is not a human review annotation.
REVIEWER ANNOTATION
A human workflow record kept separate from source facts and system classifications.
DATA_QUALITY
A retrieval, parser, provenance, or evidence-integrity failure prevented reliable evidence acquisition or interpretation.
UNKNOWN
Available system evidence does not support a determinate semantic conclusion and does not imply semantic change or safety. A successfully rescued temporary first-attempt transport failure does not by itself make the final accepted observation DATA_QUALITY.
Guarded ALERT_DETAIL final failure
A USFS ALERT_DETAIL logical request whose retrieval is FINAL_FAILED, with the required quality, provenance, retry, and no-inference shape. A rescued retry is not guarded.
Workflow action
The reviewer action recorded as a separate annotation.
REVIEWED_VALID
Confirms the displayed system classification is supported by displayed official evidence; it does not rewrite UNKNOWN, create semantic resolution, imply safety, or prevent re-evaluation when evidence changes.
REVIEWED_NOT_ACTIONABLE
Records only that no further bounded review action is needed; it does not rewrite UNKNOWN, establish known irrelevance or universal non-actionability, imply safety or road passability, treat source silence as safe, or authorize delivery.
DATA_QUALITY_HOLD
Requires an actual evidence-quality, acquisition, parser, provenance, or integrity problem preventing reliable semantic confirmation. UNKNOWN alone is not enough.
NEEDS_FOLLOWUP
Requires a concrete additional bounded investigation; it is not universally mandatory for UNKNOWN.
Transition permitted
The workflow allows a transition.
Transition required
The frozen contract imposes an obligation. The existence of an allowed transition does not by itself make that transition mandatory.
Manual acknowledgement
A human-recorded explicit acknowledgement; it is not safety or notification approval.
ACKNOWLEDGED
A terminal workflow envelope over the recorded disposition, not a semantic resolution, safety finding, notification authorization, or delivery record.
Unresolved follow-up
A condition that remains unresolved after NEEDS_FOLLOWUP; acknowledgement requires the prescribed unresolved-follow-up note and does not erase the condition.
